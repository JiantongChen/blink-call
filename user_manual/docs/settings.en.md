# Settings

## [General]-[Language]
Switch between Chinese and English display language.

## [General]-[Light/Dark Mode]
Switch between light or dark appearance. This setting only affects the display theme and does not impact software functionality.

## [Camera]-[Select Video Source]

- **Local Camera**: Use the built-in or external camera on the current computer.  
- **Remote Camera**: Use the built-in or external camera of another computer on the local network.

## [Camera]-[Start Local Camera Service]
Share the current computer's built-in or external camera with other computers on the local network.

## [Blink Call]-[Enable Blink Call]
Choose whether to enable the blink call functionality.

## [Blink Call]-[Show Progress Bar on Home Screen]
Choose to show or hide the blink call progress bar at the top of the home screen.

## [Blink Call]-[Blink Sequence]
Customize the blink action sequence with the following requirements:  

- Minimum of 1 item, can add more as needed.  
- Each item can be either eyes open or eyes closed.  
- Duration for each item ranges from 0.5 seconds to 5 seconds.  
- Default configuration: Eyes open 2 sec → Eyes closed 2 sec → Eyes open 2 sec → Eyes closed 2 sec.

## [Blink Call]-[Play Call Audio]
Choose whether to play an alert sound when a call is triggered.

## [Blink Call]-[Call Audio File]
Select different alert ringtones.

## [Blink Call]-[Audio Volume]
Adjust the audio playback volume. Note: This is the software's volume control and will not affect your system volume settings.

## [Blink Call]-[Audio Play Duration]
Set the duration for audio playback when a call is triggered:  

- Minimum duration: 10 seconds.  
- Maximum duration: Unlimited.

## [Blink Call]-[Download/Update Model Files]
Download or update the machine learning model files required by the software.

## [Other]-[Record Current Camera Data]
Start recording the video data displayed in the software and save it to a specified location (default is Desktop). Configuration details:  

- Minimum recording duration: 1 minute.  
- Maximum recording duration: 1440 minutes (24 hours).

## [Other]-[Debug Mode]
Choose whether to enable debug mode and whether to save log files locally.

## [Other]-[Restore Default Settings]
Restore all settings to their default values.
